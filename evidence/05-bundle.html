02

Where the weight went

+227.2 KB net, decomposed. Bars are to scale against the largest contributor.

1 moment.js, plus all 123 locales ▲ Avoidable +128.4 KB
56.5% of net growth · landed whole in f47b21e, 20 Jun

One function — formatRelative() in the activity feed — imports moment@2.29.4. Rollup cannot tree-shake it (dynamic require of the locale directory), so every locale from af to zh-tw ships. Locales alone are 88.1 KB of the 128.4. The app only ever renders English.

2 Icon library imported as a barrel ▲ Avoidable +61.2 KB
26.9% of net growth · landed whole in 4f10d83, 26 Jun

14 files do import { Check } from 'lucide-react'. The package index re-exports all 902 icons, and the re-export chain defeats tree-shaking under the current optimizeDeps config. 34 icons are actually used — 3.8% of what ships.

3 Charting library on the critical path ▲ Avoidable +38.5 KB
16.9% of net growth · landed whole in 33a9f0d, 30 Jun

recharts@2.7.2 (which drags in d3-scale, d3-shape, d3-array) is used by exactly one component: the seat-usage chart on /admin/seats. It is statically imported, so it sits in the main chunk. Route analytics: 1.4% of sessions ever open that page.

4 Genuine feature growth ● Expected +22.7 KB
10.0% of net growth · spread across 29 commits, none above +1.8 KB

Saved views, the command palette, SSO domain verification, CSV export, the audit-log viewer, snooze, attachment previews. This is what six weeks of shipping is supposed to look like: ~3.8 KB per week. Nothing here needs fixing.

5 Build & refactor savings already banked ▼ Recovered −23.6 KB
offsetting · 4 build commits + refactor noise

Dropping lodash.debounce (−2.8), Rollup treeshake: 'smallest' (−7.2), dropping pre-ES2020 targets (−6.4), and esbuild identifier minification (−6.2). Good work that was entirely swallowed by item 1.
